# Privacy Policy for Krubit

**Effective Date:** August 4, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 4, 2026

Krubit is a Discord application developed and operated by Krucial Studios LLC ("Krucial Studios," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how Krubit accesses, collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when installed in or used with a Discord server.

This policy applies to Krubit, its `/fetch` commands, its server-monitoring functions, and integrations that Krucial Studios may make available. It does not replace Discord's own privacy policy or the policies of third-party services connected to Krubit.

> **Development notice:** Krubit is currently in private development and shadow-canary operation. Sections describing creator feeds, member activity, entry screening, AI coordination, billing, or advanced moderation apply only if and when those features are enabled.

## 1. About Krubit

Krubit is Zariya's non-conversational Discord pet and operational companion. Krubit performs automated, rules-based tasks such as monitoring server configuration, recording factual events, checking permissions and integrations, creating configuration snapshots, and delivering functional notifications or staff reports.

Krubit is not intended to replace human community management. Zariya and authorized human staff remain responsible for community interaction, policy interpretation, contextual judgment, and consequential moderation decisions.

## 2. Information Krubit May Collect

The information Krubit processes depends on the features enabled by a server administrator and the permissions granted through Discord.

### A. Discord server and configuration data

Krubit may collect:

- Discord server identifiers and server names;
- channel, category, role, webhook, AutoMod rule, and Scheduled Event identifiers;
- channel and role names, positions, types, topics, and permission settings;
- configured notification, staff, event, and reporting channels;
- Krubit's granted, missing, and unexpected permissions;
- server settings, feature flags, and integration health information; and
- configuration snapshots, integrity hashes, and factual differences between snapshots.

Configuration snapshots do not include message history or a backup of server members.

### B. Member and staff identifiers

Krubit may collect Discord user or member identifiers when necessary to:

- record member joins and departures, which may include posting a public join
  announcement to a channel named `welcome`, if present, in addition to any
  internal staff-facing record;
- verify whether a person is authorized to use a staff command;
- record which authorized staff member requested or approved an action;
- prevent duplicate processing; and
- maintain an audit trail of bot and moderation-related actions.

### C. Operational events and audit records

Krubit may store:

- event identifiers, event types, timestamps, and guild identifiers;
- action receipts, outcomes, errors, and relevant configuration details;
- daily health-summary delivery status;
- integration failures and permission changes; and
- security, abuse-prevention, and troubleshooting logs.

Krubit applies credential-redaction safeguards before supported event details and receipts are stored.

### D. Member activity and safety data when enabled

Future or optional features may process server-local activity such as:

- account creation age, bot status, join timing, invite source where available, and join velocity;
- roles, onboarding steps, reactions, message counts, active days, event attendance, and milestone progress;
- voice-channel join and leave times or participation duration, but not voice audio or transcripts;
- public or authorized-server message links, timestamps, rule-trigger facts, and limited evidence needed to investigate spam, raids, malicious links, or other incidents; and
- staff warnings, moderation receipts, temporary watch status, and the factual reasons for a risk signal.

These features are intended to produce explainable server-safety or participation facts. Krubit is not designed to infer personality, loyalty, mental state, protected characteristics, guilt, or private relationships.

### E. Message content

Krubit does not currently read or store general message content. If a later safety, moderation, command, or workflow feature requires access to message content, Krucial Studios will limit access to the minimum necessary, document the enabled purpose, and apply configurable exclusions and retention controls.

Krucial Studios does not use Discord message content to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models without Discord's express permission.

### F. Creator and social integration data when enabled

Creator-notification features may process public or authorized information from services such as Twitch, YouTube, or other supported platforms, including creator identifiers, channel names, stream or post identifiers, titles, schedules, URLs, thumbnails, live status, and delivery records. OAuth tokens or other integration credentials may also be processed and protected as confidential secrets.

### G. Information provided directly to us

If you contact Krucial Studios for support, deletion, billing, or another request, we may receive your name, email address, Discord identifier, server identifier, correspondence, and any information you choose to provide.

If paid plans become available, payment processors may process billing and transaction information. Krucial Studios should receive only the payment status and limited billing details needed to administer the account, not full payment-card credentials.

### H. Information Krubit does not obtain from Discord

Krubit does not claim access through Discord to a member's IP address, device identifier, browser history, email address, complete cross-server history, private direct messages, or voice content.

## 3. How We Use Information

Krucial Studios may use information to:

- operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot Krubit;
- provide commands, notifications, health cards, snapshots, reports, and configured automation;
- detect duplicate events, failures, raids, spam, suspicious activity, and permission risks;
- enforce server-specific configuration and authorization rules;
- provide authorized staff with factual evidence and audit records;
- measure aggregate reliability, feature usage, and community outcomes;
- coordinate structured alerts or briefings with Zariya or other authorized systems;
- provide support and respond to privacy or deletion requests;
- administer subscriptions if paid service becomes available; and
- comply with law, Discord requirements, and platform-safety obligations.

Krucial Studios may use aggregated or de-identified information to improve Krubit when it can no longer reasonably be associated with an individual.

## 4. Automated Processing and Human Oversight

Krubit may automatically classify operational conditions or server-local safety signals using human-defined rules. Examples include missing permissions, unusual join velocity, duplicate posts, or configured AutoMod events.

Automated signals may be incorrect or incomplete. During the current and planned supervised phases, Krubit may preserve permitted evidence, increase temporary monitoring, notify staff, or recommend a reversible response. Warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, permanent role changes, or public accusations should remain subject to configured authority and human review unless a server administrator has expressly enabled a lawful, reversible automated action.

Krubit may exchange structured information with AI-assisted systems such as Zariya. Krucial Studios maintains human-defined constraints and oversight over these integrations and may restrict or disable them if needed for safety, policy compliance, or legal compliance.

## 5. How Information May Be Shared

Krucial Studios does not sell Discord API data, disclose it to data brokers or advertising networks, or use it for targeted advertising.

Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with:

- **Discord**, to operate the application through Discord's APIs and services;
- **service providers**, such as hosting, database, monitoring, support, security, or payment vendors working under appropriate obligations;
- **connected platforms**, such as Twitch or YouTube, when a server administrator enables an integration;
- **authorized server administrators or staff**, according to configured permissions and server responsibilities;
- **a third party at a user's express direction**; or
- **law enforcement, regulators, courts, or other parties**, when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and service integrity.

If Krucial Studios undergoes a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred subject to applicable law and continued protection.

## 6. Server Administrator Responsibilities

Server owners and administrators choose whether to install Krubit, which features to enable, which channels to include or exclude, and which staff members may access detailed reports. They are responsible for:

- providing notices and obtaining consent when required by applicable law;
- configuring Krubit consistently with their server rules and Discord's policies;
- limiting staff access to people with a legitimate need;
- avoiding use of Krubit for unlawful surveillance, discrimination, harassment, or sensitive eligibility decisions; and
- responding to member questions and coordinating valid requests with Krucial Studios.

Depending on applicable law and how a server uses Krubit, a server owner or administrator may independently determine the purposes of some processing and may have separate privacy obligations.

## 7. Data Retention

Krucial Studios retains information only as long as reasonably necessary for Krubit's stated functionality, security, auditability, legal obligations, and dispute resolution.

During private development and canary testing, guild configuration, events, receipts, and snapshots may be retained for the duration of the authorized test unless deletion is requested or the information is no longer needed. Before commercial member-tracking or incident-evidence features are enabled, Krucial Studios intends to provide documented retention defaults and administrator controls appropriate to those records.

Subject to legal requirements, Krucial Studios will delete or de-identify Discord API data when:

- it is no longer necessary for Krubit's permitted functionality;
- the applicable user or authorized server administrator validly requests deletion;
- Discord requests deletion;
- Krucial Studios stops operating Krubit; or
- deletion is required by law.

Residual copies may remain temporarily in protected backups until overwritten through the normal backup lifecycle.

## 8. Data Security

Krucial Studios uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, including guild-scoped storage, least-privilege permissions, staff authorization, credential redaction, TLS validation, audit receipts, and restricted operational access.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If Krucial Studios becomes aware of unauthorized access affecting Discord API data, it will investigate and provide notices to Discord, affected users, or authorities when required.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection regarding personal information, and to appeal or complain to a data-protection authority.

You may also:

- ask a server administrator which Krubit features are enabled;
- request that a server administrator exclude an eligible channel or disable optional tracking;
- remove authorized integrations you control; or
- leave a Discord server, subject to any lawful retention of existing audit or moderation records.

Krucial Studios may need to verify your identity and server relationship before fulfilling a request. Some requests may be directed to the applicable server administrator when that administrator controls the relevant configuration or record.

Krucial Studios does not knowingly use Krubit to collect personal information from a person who is prohibited from using Discord because of age. Contact us if you believe such information has been processed.
